Support

There is a person behind rqlite

rqlite has been actively developed and maintained by the same engineer since 2014. Community help is free, and commercial support and consulting are available for teams running rqlite in production.

Commercial support Community help

The maintainer

Philip O’Toole

Philip created rqlite in 2014 and has led its development ever since, through more than a decade of continuous releases. He is an Engineering Manager at Google, where he leads teams building observability infrastructure for Google Cloud.

Before Google he was Director of Engineering at InfluxData, where he was a core developer of InfluxDB, and Lead Backend Architect at Loggly. He has spent almost thirty years building distributed systems, databases, and large-scale data infrastructure, and has presented rqlite and distributed systems in Go at GopherCon and to the Carnegie Mellon Database Group.

Read more at philipotoole.com, or find him on GitHub.

Community help

Free, and the maintainer reads every message

Community support is best effort, but questions and bug reports are answered promptly.

Bug reports

Found a bug, or want to request a feature? Open an issue on GitHub. Include the rqlite version, how you run it, and the steps to reproduce.

Questions

Join the rqlite Slack to talk with the maintainer and other users, or post to the mailing list if you prefer email.

Office hours

Book a free video call to discuss your use case, a deployment you are planning, or anything else about rqlite.

Commercial support

Support and consulting from the maintainer

If your organization depends on rqlite, you can work directly with the person who wrote it. Engagements are tailored to what you are running and what you need.

  • Architecture and deployment review before you go to production, or when something has gone wrong
  • Production hardening covering security, backups, monitoring, and Kubernetes
  • Performance tuning and capacity planning for your workload
  • Prioritized bug fixes and feature development in rqlite itself
  • Training for your engineering and operations teams

Get in touch

Engagements can be a fixed-scope project or an ongoing retainer. Send a short description of how you use rqlite and what you need, and you will hear back within a couple of business days.
