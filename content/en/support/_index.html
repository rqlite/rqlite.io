Support

rqlite has been maintained by the same developer since 2014. Community help is free.

Community

Community support

Best effort, but questions and bug reports get answered.

Bug reports

Open an issue on GitHub for bugs and feature requests. Include the rqlite version, how you run it, and the steps to reproduce.

Questions

Ask on the rqlite Slack or the mailing list.

Office hours

Book a video call to discuss your use case or a deployment you are planning.

Security vulnerabilities

Report privately on GitHub or email hello@philipotoole.com.

Commercial

Working with the maintainer

Some organizations need a named engineer under contract before they can run software in production. That is available.

  • Architecture and deployment review
  • Production hardening covering security, backups, monitoring, and Kubernetes
  • Performance tuning and capacity planning
  • Bug fixes and feature development on a defined timeline
  • Training for engineering and operations teams

Contact

Fixed-scope projects or an ongoing retainer. Email a short description of how you use rqlite and what you need.

The maintainer

Philip O’Toole

Philip created rqlite and has led its development ever since, through more than a decade of continuous releases. He has spent over fifteen years building distributed systems, databases, and large-scale data infrastructure. He has presented rqlite and distributed systems at GopherCon, spoken at AWS re:Invent on large-scale log aggregation and analytics, and presented rqlite to the Carnegie Mellon Database Group.

Read more at philipotoole.com, or find him on GitHub.
