Support

rqlite has been maintained by the same engineer since 2014. Community help is free. Paid support and consulting are available.

Paid support Community help

The maintainer

Philip O’Toole

Philip created rqlite in 2014 and has led its development ever since, through more than a decade of continuous releases. He has spent over fifteen years building distributed systems, databases, and large-scale data infrastructure. He has presented rqlite and distributed systems in Go at GopherCon, spoken at AWS re:Invent on large-scale log aggregation and analytics, and presented rqlite to the Carnegie Mellon Database Group.

Read more at philipotoole.com, or find him on GitHub.

Bug reports

Questions

Ask on the rqlite Slack or the mailing list.

Office hours
